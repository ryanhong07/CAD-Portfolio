### 3. Modular Kinematic Mechanism (Marble Run Module)
* **Domain:** Kinematic Mechanisms, Dynamics, Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM)[cite: 17, 19, 24]
* **CAD & Tools:** SOLIDWORKS, DfAM Optimization, Dynamic Equilibrium Modeling[cite: 17, 19, 24]
* **Key Achievements:**
  * Engineered a passive, self-resetting tipping bucket mechanism governed by dynamic torque equilibrium:
    $$\sum \tau_{\text{pivot}} = (m_{\text{marble}} \cdot g \cdot x_{\text{marble}}) - (m_{\text{bucket}} \cdot g \cdot x_{\text{bucket}})$$[cite: 19]
  * Tuned a 14.00 mm pivot height and a 9.83-degree bottom slope to ensure positive tipping under load and instantaneous self-righting upon marble ejection[cite: 19].
  * Applied DfAM principles (45-degree overhang chamfers, teardrop self-supporting apertures, optimal build-orientation datum faces), reducing print time by **51 minutes** and eliminating difficult internal support structures[cite: 24, 25].
